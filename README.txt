Files

Publish
1. Upload the complete extracted folder to your hosting provider (for example, Netlify).
2. Open the public website URL and test all phone, WhatsApp and email links.
3. Add the published URL to Google Search Console and verify ownership.
4. Use URL Inspection > Request indexing for the homepage.
5. Once your final domain is known, create a sitemap.xml using the final canonical URL and add its URL in Search Console. Do not submit a sitemap with a placeholder domain.
6. Add/claim Google Business Profile and keep the business name, phone, address/service area consistent. Ask real customers for genuine reviews.
